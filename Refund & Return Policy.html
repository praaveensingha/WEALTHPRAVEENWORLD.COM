Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy – Praveenworld.com:

Last Updated: August 20, 2025

At wealth.Praveenworld.com, we strive to provide transformational learning experiences through our courses, coaching, workshops, and digital products. Since our offerings are primarily digital and service-based, we have set clear guidelines for refunds, returns, and cancellations to maintain transparency.

1. Digital Products (Courses, E-books, Training Materials)

All digital products (online courses, e-books, downloadable materials) are non-returnable and non-refundable once accessed, downloaded, or delivered.

If you face technical issues that prevent access, please contact help@praveenworld.com
. We will resolve the issue promptly.

2. Coaching, Mentorship & Consultation Services

All coaching, mentorship, and consultation bookings are non-refundable once confirmed.

Rescheduling: You may reschedule a booked session by notifying us at least 24 hours in advance.

No-shows or last-minute cancellations are not eligible for refunds or rescheduling.

3. Subscription & Membership Programs

Subscriptions and memberships may be canceled anytime, but cancellations will apply to the next billing cycle.

No partial or prorated refunds will be issued for unused days in the current subscription period.

4. Workshops, Live Events & Training Programs

Payments for workshops, webinars, and live training sessions are non-refundable once registration is confirmed.

If you are unable to attend, you may:

Request a transfer of your registration to a future event, OR

Nominate someone else to attend in your place (by prior notice).

5. Exceptional Refund Cases

Refunds will only be granted under the following conditions:

Duplicate payment made due to a technical error.

Payment deducted but access not granted, and the issue remains unresolved.

In such cases, refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

6. Cancellation Policy

Orders for digital products cannot be canceled once placed, as access is granted instantly.

Service or session cancellations must follow the rules mentioned in section (2).

For subscriptions, cancellation ensures no further billing from the next cycle.

7. Contact Information

For refund, return, or cancellation-related queries, please reach us at:

📧 Email: help@praveenworld.com

🌐 Website: wealth.praveenworld.com

🔒 Disclaimer:

By purchasing from wealth.Praveenworld.com, you agree to this Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy. This ensures fair usage and helps us maintain the integrity of our services and digital content.
